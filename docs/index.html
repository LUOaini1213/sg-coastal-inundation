Singapore coastal inundation explorer

Still-water flooding from extreme sea levels plus IPCC AR6 sea-level rise, with hydraulic connectivity.

Screening study, not an official flood map. Coastal defences, drainage, waves and rainfall are not modelled, and the 30 m DEM is a surface model (buildings and trees included). Independent personal project; not affiliated with NUS, TMSI or PUB.
